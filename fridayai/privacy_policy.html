Information Collection and Use

To provide the features you use, we process the content you submit and information about your use of the Service. AI requests are sent to our backend and the third-party AI provider described below; this processing is not limited to your device.

AI Features and Third-Party Processing

Copilot AI uses OpenAI through our backend to provide chat, image understanding, document questions and answers, translation, image generation and editing, and web-assisted answers. Depending on the feature you use, the following information is sent to OpenAI:

  • Chat: the message you submit, relevant earlier messages and AI replies from the conversation, and instructions such as the selected assistant role and response language.
  • Images and documents: images you select or take, including reference images used again from the conversation, and the filenames and text extracted from documents you submit. Relevant earlier document content may be included in later requests.
  • Translation: the text to translate and the selected source and target languages. Text entered through voice input is included when you submit it.
  • Image generation and editing: your prompt, requested image size, and any reference images supplied for the request.
  • Web-assisted answers: the search query and relevant conversation context, which can include submitted text, images, and document content, for processing with OpenAI's web search service.
  • Safety checks: submitted text and images may also be sent to OpenAI for content moderation. Our backend may send OpenAI a pseudonymous safety identifier derived from the app-specific device identifier to help detect and prevent abuse.

These submissions can contain personal information, such as names, contact details, faces in images, or private information in documents, if you include it. That information is transmitted as part of the submitted content; it is not automatically removed before processing.

Our backend also receives an app-specific device identifier, usage counts, app version, language settings, and subscription status to operate the Service and manage usage. This information is distinct from the content and safety identifier sent to OpenAI above.

Voice input uses Apple's Speech framework. Audio may be sent to Apple for speech recognition. When you submit the recognized text for an AI feature, that text is sent to our backend and OpenAI; the app does not send the original voice recording to OpenAI.
